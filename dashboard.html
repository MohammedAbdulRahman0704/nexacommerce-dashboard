PROBLEM 1
Retention Cliff at Month 3
−11.8pp
M+1 retention: 21.4% → M+3: 9.6%. Consistent across all 27 cohorts — structural issue, not seasonal. No post-purchase communication in the 21–45 day window.
SOLUTION 1
3-Touch Re-engagement Drip
+4.4pp target
Day 7: Order follow-up + cross-sell. Day 21: ML-ranked "You might also like". Day 45: 10% loyalty incentive (time-limited). Industry benchmark: +4–7pp M+3 lift.
IMPACT 1
Retention Revenue Recovery
$1.2M–$2.1M
~12 additional retained customers per cohort × avg $2,800 LTV = $33,600/cohort. Annualised across 12 monthly cohorts. Conservative industry estimate applied.
PROBLEM 2
70.9% Overall Churn Rate
$2.98M at risk
500 customers idle 45–90 days. Email campaigns churn worst (72.4%) — attracting deal-seekers. VIP segment churns at only 52.1% despite highest LTV.
SOLUTION 2
Win-Back + Loyalty Programme
35% recovery target
Personalised "We miss you" email + 15% discount to 500 at-risk customers. Tiered loyalty points for VIP + Premium (25% of base, 68% of revenue).
IMPACT 2
Churn Reduction Revenue
$1.04M recovered
Win-back: 175 customers × avg $5,970 recovered LTV = $1.04M. Loyalty programme: churn 70.9% → 62% = +854 active customers × $2,200 avg = $1.88M/yr.
PROBLEM 3
10.8% Cart Abandonment
175 lost/month
Users dropping at checkout_start → checkout_complete. Forced account creation is #1 UX barrier. 6+ field form with no autofill or progress indicator.
SOLUTION 3
Checkout UX Overhaul
2-step flow
Enable guest checkout. Compress to 2-step: shipping → payment. Add Google Places address autocomplete. Add progress bar. Estimated 1–2 week engineering effort.
IMPACT 3
Checkout Revenue Recovery
$322K/year
Recover 25% of 10.8% abandon rate = 44 extra completions/month × avg $610 AOV × 12 months. Industry: guest checkout reduces abandon by 25–35%.
